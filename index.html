Phantom Chase: Privacy Policy

Alku Studios · Last updated: 15 October 2026

Phantom Chase is a single-player game for Windows. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any, and it keeps nothing anywhere but on your own PC.

What we collect

Phantom Chase does not collect, receive or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no online features. It makes no network connections at all: it works fully offline, and nothing it stores is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps on your PC

To remember your progress, the game writes a few small files to a folder on your own computer, under your user profile:

  • your save: coins, purchases, high scores, statistics, mission progress, settings and key bindings;
  • a history of your finished runs, which the game's own statistics screen reads;
  • a short log of how your display was set up, for working out a screen problem;
  • crash reports, if the game ever crashes (see below).

These files stay on your PC. The game's Settings screen can erase your progress, and uninstalling the game from the Microsoft Store normally removes them too. You can also delete the files yourself at any time.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it saves a crash report on your PC. The report contains the game's version, your Windows version and machine type, what the game was doing (which screen, which mode, how far you had run), the technical error trace and the end of the display log. It can include file paths, and those may contain your Windows user name.

A crash report never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it. If you pick the option to email it, the game copies the report to your clipboard and opens a message in your own email program, addressed to us, for you to paste it into and send. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you can read or change it first. If you do send one, we use it only to find and fix the fault, and we do not share it.

The Microsoft Store

If you get Phantom Chase from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, the installation and any purchase, and may collect information about them and about app crashes and usage under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We see only the summary reports Microsoft shows developers, which do not identify you.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and has no chat, no online play and nothing to purchase with real money.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to collect or send anything, this page will say so, and the date at the top will change before that version is released.
